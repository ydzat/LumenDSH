Design review · not implementation evidence

Native Android layout target: 360 dp wide. 16 dp side margins, 15–16 sp body copy, 44–48 dp primary touch targets. The composer stays above the keyboard.

Appearance occupies a small reserved slot. The geometric mark is an original placeholder, not final character artwork. Conversation, voice and permissions remain independent.

Connection details, pairing and recovery stay available under the menu. Pending decisions remain visible in the conversation. A skin cannot obscure them.
