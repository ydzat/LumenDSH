## Objective and boundary

character habitat and native client capabilities. Prefer extraction from the pinned
Android reference (`a5af7b105b7fdb006e3fc3be2a2179d83907c2cb`) and its tests. Maintain
one agent/session, independent Termux DSH, the approved cyberspace design, separate
appearance/voice/behavior settings and local-only releases.

The user-requested expansion includes bounded share intake, notification history
and same-session local scheduling, previously only candidates in the audit. It does
not include broad notification/clipboard monitoring, finance/account polling,
virtual displays, remote access or all bundled upstream skills.

Source incompatibility must lead to an adapted implementation of the required
behavior, or an explicit unresolved gate; a research note alone cannot close it.
Do not replace working components merely to increase copied code. Record source,
revision, license, extracted symbols, adaptations and actual validation per work item.
